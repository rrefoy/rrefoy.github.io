Work

Most consultancies specialize in one piece of the delivery chain: strategy, design, or engineering. I specialize in the piece that connects them — the delivery management, the vendor relationship, and the internal team capability that determine whether good ideas survive contact with a real government system.

What I Offer

Delivery & Program Management

Embedded or fractional delivery leadership for digital service engagements — risk management, cross-team alignment, milestone accountability, and the daily work of keeping a complex, multi-stakeholder project on track.

Team & Capability Enablement

Building the internal muscle — standards, hiring, onboarding, and delivery norms — so an organization or team can keep running agile, human-centered practices after an engagement ends, rather than becoming dependent on outside help.

Agile Acquisitions & Vendor De-risking

Translating between agile delivery practice and government procurement — writing and shaping RFQs, structuring performance-based contracts, and helping agencies buy and manage technology work in a way that supports iterative delivery.

Service Design & HCD Facilitation

Discovery, stakeholder mapping, and journey work that turns research into a plan leadership can actually act on — applying human-centered design methods (Discover, Decide, Make, Validate) without pretending to be a design specialist.

How I Work

Listen First

Every engagement starts with the people doing the work, not just the people who hired me.

Make the Plan Visible

Findings only matter once they're something a whole group can see and agree on — a roadmap, a sequenced plan, a shared definition of success.

Deliver, Don't Just Advise

I run the work, not just recommend it — milestones, risk management, and stakeholder alignment through to launch.

Leave It Working

The goal isn't dependency. Every engagement should leave the team with the standards, skills, and confidence to keep going without me.
