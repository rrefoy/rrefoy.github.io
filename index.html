I help government agencies and mission-driven organizations get complicated public-service technology work actually delivered — not just planned, designed, or contracted for, but shipped, adopted, and sustained.

I'm a delivery generalist who moves fluidly between strategy and execution: running programs, shaping acquisitions, building team capacity, and facilitating the human-centered work that makes services actually work for the people who depend on them.

I approach problems with curiosity, build for scale, and lead with flexibility and purpose. I care deeply about doing this work in ways that are inclusive, psychologically safe, and give people back their time — because I believe that even small improvements to public services can meaningfully improve people's lives.
