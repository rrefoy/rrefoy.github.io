Senior Product & Program Leader

Rebecca Refoy

I help government agencies and mission-driven organizations get complicated public-service technology work actually delivered — not just planned, designed, or contracted for, but shipped, adopted, and sustained after the launch date passes.

View Services

Services

Most consultancies specialize in one piece of the delivery chain: strategy, design, or engineering. I specialize in the piece that connects them — the delivery management, the vendor relationship, and the internal team capability that determine whether good ideas survive contact with a real government system.

Who This Is For

Agency Leaders

Who have the mandate and the budget to modernize a service, but need someone who can actually run the engagement day to day — not just advise from the outside.

Civic Tech Firms & Prime Contractors

Who need a senior delivery lead or acquisitions-savvy partner to embed on a specific engagement.

Tribal, State & Local Partners

Navigating complex, under-resourced systems (health IT, benefits, education) where the technology is rarely the hardest part.

Nonprofits & Foundations

Funding technology-enabled public services who need an outside read on whether an initiative is set up to succeed.

What I Offer

1

Delivery & Program Management

Embedded or fractional delivery leadership for digital service engagements — risk management, cross-team alignment, milestone accountability, and the daily work of keeping a complex, multi-stakeholder project on track.

Proof Points

Delivery Manager for IRS Direct File (140,000 pilot users, 80%+ increase in user trust, GSA's 2024 Customer Experience Award); led delivery across 7 product teams and 2 software platforms; managed $10M+ digital transformation portfolios at 18F.

2

Team & Capability Enablement

Building the internal muscle — standards, hiring, onboarding, and delivery norms — so an organization or team can keep running agile, human-centered practices after an engagement ends, rather than becoming dependent on outside help.

Proof Points

As Chief Delivery Officer at LakeRaven LLC, built the organization's contractor handbook, HR documentation, job descriptions, onboarding checklists, and delivery standards while leading tribal health IT engagements.

3

Agile Acquisitions & Vendor De-risking

Translating between agile delivery practice and government procurement — writing and shaping RFQs, structuring performance-based contracts, and helping agencies buy and manage technology work in a way that supports iterative delivery.

Proof Points

Co-editor of 18F's federal field guide to de-risking government technology; decade-long record of published writing on agile acquisitions; FAC-COR III certified; led RFQ ghostwriting and acquisition strategy for federal agencies.

4

Service Design & HCD Facilitation

Discovery, stakeholder mapping, and journey work that turns research into a plan leadership can actually act on — applying human-centered design methods (Discover, Decide, Make, Validate) without pretending to be a design specialist.

Proof Points

Directed discovery and stakeholder research for statewide gap analysis engagements; has applied human-centered design methods since 2013, beginning in the Peace Corps in Guinea.

How I Work

Listen First

Every engagement starts with the people doing the work, not just the people who hired me.

Make the Plan Visible

Findings only matter once they're something a whole group can see and agree on — a roadmap, a sequenced plan, a shared definition of success.

Deliver, Don't Just Advise

I run the work, not just recommend it — milestones, risk management, and stakeholder alignment through to launch.

Leave It Working

The goal isn't dependency. Every engagement should leave the team with the standards, skills, and confidence to keep going without me.
