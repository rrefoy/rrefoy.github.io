Services

Most consultancies focus on strategy, design, or engineering. I focus on what connects them: delivery management, vendor relationships, and building your team's skills. This decides if good ideas actually work in government.

Who I Work With

Agency Leaders

You have the budget to modernize a service. You need someone to run the project day to day — not just advise from outside.

Civic Tech Firms & Contractors

You need a senior delivery lead who understands acquisitions to join your team on a project.

Tribal, State & Local Partners

You're working in complex systems (health IT, benefits, education) where the technology is rarely the hardest part.

Nonprofits & Foundations

You fund public-service technology and want an outside view on whether a project is set up to succeed.

What I Offer

1

Delivery & Program Management

Team & Capability Building

Agile Acquisitions & Vendor Management

Service Design & Research

Track Record

How I Work

Listen First

I start with the people doing the work, not just the people who hired me.

Make the Plan Visible

Findings matter when everyone can see them — a roadmap, a clear plan, a shared definition of success.

Do the Work

I run the project, not just advise on it — milestones, risks, and stakeholder alignment through launch.

Leave You Stronger

The goal isn't to make you depend on me. I leave your team with the skills and confidence to keep going.
